# SQ RESCUE (2026-06-11) — rolling curve refit, regime gate, concurrency cap, live-physics verdict

**Question.** The stale-quote family (`det_sqp_v1/v2`) is the biggest paper earner
(v1 +$1,158 / 1,924 trades, v2 +$1,648 / 1,345 trades as of 06-11) but has NO live
path because of three documented blockers: a frozen fair-value curve that drifted
(A3: 8.4× calibration error, +5.6pp Up-bias by 06-04), a structural bleed in
directional tape (lwd↔sq anti-correlation; 4-coin macro-correlated variance), and
execution fragility (A2, 06-04: realistic fills kept only 43% of EV, CI crossing 0 —
measured before the live-calibrated fill model existed). Can the family be rescued
into a shadow-live candidate?

**Answer.** **One blocker fixed and shipped, one honestly negative, verdict
NEEDS-MORE.** (1) The frozen curve was worse than documented — it went **negative
OOS** (−$0.47/tr, WR 38%, Chainlink-settled) while the paper engine's
Coinbase-settled idealized view still showed +$1.6/tr; the **rolling 3-day refit
fixes calibration 5× and flips v1 to +$0.60** (paired Δ +$0.98 [+0.43,+1.52]),
and a **drift alarm (τ=0.0414)** catches the documented incident 5 days early
with zero false alarms → ADOPTED. (2) The dev-fit BTC-trendiness regime gate is
an **OOS no-op** (keep-rate 98.3%) → honest negative; the lwd↔sq bleed remains
unmodeled. (3) A C=2 concurrency cap was rejected by the registered d+h chooser
but would have passed its future legs (0.57× day-sd at −3% total) → queued for
re-test. (4) Under the live-calibrated fill model at $5 the family earns
**+$0.27–0.45/fill seeds-mean with every CI spanning zero** on the 4.2-day
future block (registered bar: CI-lower>0 ∧ EV≥$0.50); the lone seed-0 "pass"
(+$0.97) dissolved into $0.39±0.34 across seeds. ~45–65% of the family's
apparent paper EV was settlement-oracle + fill-model artifact. **Not
shadow-live; re-judge on a doubled future block (~5 more days) with the
pre-built pipeline.**

## 1. Protocol

- **Splits (LC2 convention):** dev 05-23..27 / holdout 05-28..06-04 / **future
  06-05..09** (06-09 partial to ~05:00 UTC), revealed exactly once. 06-01..04 was
  burned by earlier campaigns' reveals → holdout here. Declared prior exposure of
  06-05..09: the live fill model's COST parameters are calibrated on it (physics
  only, never sees outcomes/direction); no sq-family entry-rule EV had ever been
  computed on it before this reveal.
- **Decision semantics** = `StaleQuoteState` live parity: first qualifying tick per
  window; `margin ≤ |model_p − yes_mid| ≤ 0.30`; `|spot_vel_10s| ≥ 8bps`; ask band
  [0.05, 0.95] + depth ≥ stake pre-checks at the DECISION tick; v2 = margin 0.12 +
  dist ≤ 19bps. **Exits: hold-to-resolution** — verified against the paper record
  (3,269/3,269 sq trades have `exit_reason="resolution"`), so no early-exit modeling
  is needed. The $1/share settle convention ran ~$0.09/win optimistic on live $5
  det trades ([[live-pnl-groundtruth-vs-book]]; the ledger registration's "~$0.04"
  understated this — corrected here, no gate keys on it).
- **Walk-forward arms (SQR1–3):** $10 stake, real-L2 `walk_buy` at entry+2s, trade
  dropped if unfilled > 50% — continuity with `sq_full.parquet`. Settlement:
  **Chainlink primary** (`cl_outcomes`, the oracle Polymarket pays), Coinbase
  reported for paper parity.
- **Live re-judgment (SQR4):** `rejudge_live_model.simulate_config` pattern at
  **$5**, `live_guarded` (`fill_model_live.json` live-1, 246 attempts: sampled
  empirical latency, zero-fill hazard by depth×time-left, kappa 1.056, guard floor
  entry−0.04), **chase ceiling = min(entry_ask+0.07, 0.95)** (registered: the
  family's 0.95 max_ask is an entry filter, not a chase band), seed-0 headline +
  seeds 0–4, fixed-latency {2,5,10}s sensitivity.
- **CIs:** `window_clustered_bootstrap` n=2000 seed 0 — slug-clustered (repo
  convention, gates) AND epoch-clustered (`window_start_ts`; sq variance is
  macro-correlated, so slug clusters understate; a slug-pass that fails
  epoch-clustered downgrades the verdict one band).
- **Memory constraints honored:** no tight daily caps (H4: costs 33–48% of sq), no
  deep-tail min-price floor (H3: the ~0.17 longshots are the jackpots). Shaping
  tools = regime gate + concurrency cap only.

## 2. Builder reconciliation (dev+holdout, before any future compute)

My decision builder is `StaleQuoteState` live parity; the old `sq_full.parquet`
builder (`build_full_ledgers.sq_ledger_parity`) differs in two stated ways: it

new n=1,471 @ +$1.46/tr, slug overlap 66%. The drop is dominated by the live
depth pre-check — i.e. the old ledger counted trades the live bot would have
refused. Same family, same economics; all campaign numbers use the live-parity
builder.

## 3. SQR1 — frozen curve vs rolling refit (dev+holdout walk-forward)

Chainlink-settled, $10 L2 walk at +2s, common eligible days 05-26..06-04 (first
3-day trail completes 05-26; K=5/7 arms cover fewer days — stated):

| arm | n | EV/tr (CL) | 90% CI (slug) |
|---|---|---|---|
| frozen-original (.bak, deployed until 06-05) | 1,298 | **+$0.43** | [−0.30, +1.22] ✗ crosses 0 |
| rolling 2d, daily refit | 1,290 | +$0.85 | [+0.23, +1.46] |
| **rolling 3d, daily refit (primary)** | 1,296 | **+$1.16** | **[+0.51, +1.83]** |
| rolling 5d, daily refit | 1,114 | +$0.66 | [−0.06, +1.43] |
| rolling 7d, daily refit | 926 | +$0.07 | [−0.72, +0.90] |

The frozen curve is **not significantly positive on Chainlink** over these 10
days; the 3-day rolling refit roughly **triples EV/trade** and clears zero. The
window-length sweep is single-peaked at K=3 (K=2 noisy fit, K≥5 stale fit —
exactly the drift-speed story). Cadence 1/2/3 days are statistically
indistinguishable; the registered rule ("cheapest within $0.10/tr of daily")
selects **refit every 3 days**, and since a daily refit is a free cron job the
operational recommendation is: refit daily, alarm regardless (below).

**Drift alarm (registered spec).** A(d) = decision-z-density-weighted mean
|p_inuse − p_fresh3d(d)| on the z-grid, computable at end of day d. Normal-churn
envelope under the rolling-3d curve on dev+holdout: **τ = 0.0414**. The
frozen-original curve's A(d) crosses τ on **2026-05-31** and stays above through
06-04 — i.e. the alarm would have flagged the documented A3 drift incident **5
days before the manual 06-05 refit**, with zero false alarms on the rolling arm
by construction.

## 6. Verdict

**Per the registered bands: NEEDS-MORE — not a shadow-live candidate today, not
a reject.**

What is SHIPPED out of this campaign (the durable fix):

- **Rolling 3-day curve refit + drift alarm (SQR1, adopted).** The frozen curve
  didn't just decay — it went NEGATIVE (−$0.47/tr OOS at WR 38%) while staying
  +$1.6/tr in the Coinbase-settled idealized view the paper engine reports. The
  rolling refit fixes calibration 5× (WCE 0.0703 → 0.0138), flips v1 EV
  (paired Δ +$0.98 [+0.43, +1.52]), and the alarm (τ=0.0414 on the
  decision-density-weighted curve shift vs a fresh 3-day fit) would have caught
  the documented incident on 05-31 — five days before the hand refit actually
  happened — with zero false alarms. Refit cadence: every ≤3 days (registered
  pick: 3d; daily is operationally free). This should be wired into the paper
  strategies regardless of any live decision: the currently-deployed curve is a
  one-off hand refit that will rot exactly like the original did.

What is NOT fixed:

- **The regime bleed (SQR2): honest negative.** Dev-fit BTC-trendiness
  thresholds never trigger OOS (keep 98.3%). The lwd↔sq anti-correlation
  remains real and unmodeled.
- **Concurrency (SQR3): chooser said no.** On the future block a C=2 cap was
  nearly free (−3% total) at 0.57× day-sd — re-test as a risk control when more
  days exist; not shipped now.
- **The live-physics economics (SQR4).** At $5 under the live-calibrated fill
  model the whole family earns ~+$0.27–0.45/fill (seeds-mean) with every CI
  spanning zero on 4.2 future days, against a registered bar of CI-lower > 0
  and EV ≥ $0.50. For scale: ~85–145 fills/day means even +$0.3/fill ≈
  $25–45/day — the volume is the family's redeeming feature, but per-fill
  significance is nowhere near established, and the number sits inside the
  fill-model's known optimism wedge.

**The single biggest remaining risk** (why needs-more and not a hopeful deploy):
**adverse selection of the zero-fill hazard.** The model skips ~44–49% of
trades at RANDOM; live, the misses concentrate in winners (the A2/live-gap
attribution finding — sq is a momentum-chasing taker racing the book's
repricing, so the fills you actually get are the ones the market was happy to
give you). A seeds-mean of +$0.3/fill fits entirely inside that optimism wedge —
true live EV could be ~$0. No backtest can close this; only supervised live
fills (or the executor's touch-start guards) can measure it.

**Recommended path:**
1. Wire the rolling refit + alarm into the paper sq strategies now (zero-risk,
   pure data-quality fix; the engine already reads `curve_file` at boot).
2. Keep `det_sqp_*` paper-only. Re-run `sq_rescue.py --stage reveal` after ~5
   more clean days (the future block then doubles): promote to shadow-live IFF
   the rescued config's seeds-mean CI clears zero at $5 and 5s-latency EV holds.
3. Re-test the C=2 concurrency cap on that extended block as a risk gate.
4. If promoted, first live week at $5 with the existing per-strategy bankroll
   convention ($100 bankroll), explicitly NO tight daily cap (H4) and NO
   min-price floor (H3).

## 7. Approximations & caveats (stated, not hidden)

1. **Exits.** Modeled as hold-to-resolution settles at $1/share — this IS the
   deployed `StaleQuoteState` behavior (verified on all 3,269 paper trades), not an
   approximation of some richer exit policy. The $1-settle convention measured
   ~$0.09/win optimistic vs actual redeemed cash on live $5 det trades.
2. **Zero-fill hazard is random in the model, adversely selected in life.** The
   live attribution showed missed trades skew winners; live_guarded EV is therefore
   an optimistic bound exactly as documented for every other config judged under
   this model. sq buys INTO a fresh spot jump (momentum-chasing taker), so its
   misses plausibly skew the same way.
3. **Curve fit target is the Coinbase outcome** (`outcome_up_clean`) — deployed
   parity — while EV is settled on Chainlink. A cl_up-fit curve is a plausible
   refinement, deliberately NOT tested here (one more selection knob; the rolling
   machinery makes it a one-line change later).
4. **The regime-gate feature fails OPEN** when BTC spot coverage < 60% of the
   trailing window (4.1% of dev decisions): data gaps are not evidence of chop.
5. **06-09 is a partial day** (to ~05:00 UTC) — it contributes ~1/5 weight to the
   future block; per-day breakdowns in the artifacts.
6. **Slug- vs epoch-clustered CIs.** sq fires multi-coin on one macro move; the
   epoch-clustered CI is the honest variance floor and is reported everywhere; the
   verdict downgrades if it disagrees (registered).
7. **Multiple-testing surface.** The K/cadence sweep (7 arms) and gate grid (12
   cells) were selected on dev(+holdout) only; the future block graded ONE
   pre-chosen config per question. The K-sweep's K≥5 arms cover fewer eligible
   d+h days than K≤3 (trail length); the day-matched cadence comparison is the
   decision-relevant one.
